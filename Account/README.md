# Requesting an Account

To request an account, send an email to dsi-recherche_AT_univ-paris13.fr.

If you are not affiliated with USPN, you must have a sponsor with a USPN email address who can validate your registration. The easiest way is to include your sponsor in CC when sending your request.

Once your MAGI account has been created, you will receive two emails from dsi-recherche_AT_univ-paris13.fr.

## Password initialization email

The first email has the subject:

   > **Update Your account**

and contains the following message:

   > Your administrator has just requested that you update your Magi account by performing the following action(s): Update Password. Click on the link below to start this process.
   >
   > Link to account update
   >
   > This link will expire within 1 day.
   >
   > If you are unaware that your administrator has requested this, just ignore this message and nothing will be changed.

This email contains a link that allows you to initialize your password.

## Account creation email

The second email has the subject:

   > **Your MAGI account has been created**

It contains your username and is similar to the following:

   > Hello,
   >
   > Your MAGI account has been created.
   >
   > Username: nico  
   > Account portal: https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr/realms/magi/account/
   >
   > You will receive a separate email from the MAGI account portal containing a personal link to set your password. For security reasons, this link is generated and sent directly by Keycloak.
   >
   > Best regards,  
   > MAGI / DSI Research Support Team

# Magi Account Management

The interface for managing your Magi account is available here:

[https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr)

From this interface, you can:

- Change your password
- Reset your password
- Update your contact email address
- Update your personal information (First Name / Last Name)

## Changing your password

1. Sign in at [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr).
2. Once logged in, go to **Account security** in the left-hand menu.
3. Select **Signing in**.
4. In the **Basic authentication** section, select **My password**, then click **Update**.
5. You will be asked to authenticate again to make sure that you are the person requesting the password change.
6. Once authenticated, enter your new password, confirm it, and click **Submit**.

## I forgot my password

1. Go to [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr).
2. Click **Forgot Password?**
3. Enter either your username or your contact email address, then click **Submit**.
4. After clicking **Submit**, wait a few seconds until the following confirmation message appears:

   > You should receive an email shortly with further instructions.

5. Check your mailbox. You should receive an email from dsi-recherche_AT_univ-paris13.fr with the subject **Reset password**. The email contains a password-reset link pointing to [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr), followed by a reset token.
6. Open the link. You will be redirected to a page asking you to enter and confirm your new password. Then click **Submit**.

## Updating your contact email address

1. Go to [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr).
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Click **Update email** next to the **Email** field.
4. You will be asked to authenticate again to make sure that you are the person requesting the change.
5. Once authenticated, enter your new email address and click **Submit**.
6. Wait a few seconds until the following message appears:

   > Confirmation email sent

7. Check the mailbox associated with your new email address. You should receive an email from dsi-recherche_AT_univ-paris13.fr with the subject **Verify new email**. The message contains a link pointing to [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr), followed by a long verification token. Click the link.
8. Your request is then validated and your contact email address is updated.

## Updating your personal information

1. Go to [https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr](https://magiaccount.univ-spn.fr).
2. Sign in to your account.
3. You can directly edit the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields, then click **Save**.
